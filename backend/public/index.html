How it works

  1. Your phone creates a secret code. Only its fingerprint is sent to the seller.
  2. You pay. The money is locked: the seller can see it but cannot take it.
  3. The rider arrives, you check the package and show your code.
  4. Seller and rider are paid in the same second.
  5. No delivery before the deadline? Automatic refund.
